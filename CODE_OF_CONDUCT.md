# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is open to everyone. We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps the community:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback — reviews critique the code, not the person.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Behavior that is not tolerated:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, public or private.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and code review — and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the maintainer through GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainer is obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face repercussions as determined by the project's leadership.
